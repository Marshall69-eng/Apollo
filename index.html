How a site gets chosen

The path from raw sensor data to a deployment decision.

Sense

Solar-powered buoys anchored at candidate points log flow velocity, debris density and water level continuously.

Score & rank

Readings run through a siting model that ranks every candidate against deployment thresholds.

Deploy & monitor

Crews are dispatched to the top-ranked site; sensors stay active to confirm performance and flag drift.

Site-selection criteria: flow velocity 0.5–3+ m/s, high debris accumulation, proximity to flood-prone esteros and vulnerable communities, solar and signal access for continuous monitoring.

What this dashboard does

  • Real-time monitoring of river flow, flood risk and plastic load.
  • Predictive, flood-based deployment scheduling across units.
  • Remote ram control, alerts and maintenance scheduling.
  • Shared data feed for LGU disaster-response teams and DENR.

Impact

LGUs & communities  Earlier flood warnings and clearer waterways in high-risk barangays.

Economic  Revenue recovered from baled plastic sold to recyclers.

Environmental  Less flood-linked plastic blockage and less ocean-bound leakage.
