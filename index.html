What this dashboard does

  • Real-time monitoring of river flow, flood risk and plastic load.
  • Predictive, flood-based deployment scheduling across units.
  • Remote ram control, alerts and maintenance scheduling.
  • Automatic Telegram and SMS dispatch to Barangay and LGU DRRMO duty officers.
  • Shared data feed for LGU disaster-response teams and DENR.

Impact

LGUs and communitiesEarlier flood warnings and clearer waterways in high-risk barangays.

EconomicRevenue recovered from baled plastic sold to recyclers.

EnvironmentalLess flood-linked plastic blockage and less ocean-bound leakage.

Dispatch thresholds

A site that breaches either threshold triggers an automatic notification.

Locked

Thresholds are safety constants defined in main.py and served read-only, so nobody can raise a limit during an event. Changing one is a reviewed code change.

Dry-run channels still record and display alerts, but nothing leaves the server until TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN or the Twilio variables are set in .env.

Notification recipients

Barangay and LGU DRRMO duty officers on the escalation list.
